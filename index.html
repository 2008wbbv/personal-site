I build wireless brain–computer interfaces and radar systems.

Incoming @ MIT BWSI · SWE @ Synthica · AI research @ MIT Mantis AI · Rev A @ Solderable · Hack Club × NASA ambassador

I work at the seam between neural interfaces and sensing. Right now that means synthetic-aperture radar flown on unmanned aircraft at MIT Lincoln Laboratory's Beaver Works Summer Institute, a wireless BCI platform at Solderable, training-data pipelines for an AI systems engineer at Mantis AI (MIT CSAIL), and research-publication infrastructure at Synthica.

Away from the bench I captain a SeaPerch robotics team, organize hackathons as a Hack Club × NASA Stardance ambassador, and led a youth grant program that moved $10k to local nonprofits.
