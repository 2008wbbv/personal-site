I am a researcher and engineer in Boston working on sensing and machine learning systems.
At **MIT Lincoln Laboratory's Beaver Works Summer Institute** I study synthetic-aperture
radar carried on unmanned aircraft; at **Mantis AI (MIT CSAIL)** I build training-data
pipelines for AI systems engineering; at **Synthica** I maintain research-publication
infrastructure; and at **Solderable** I develop a wireless brain–computer interface
platform under a Rev A hardware grant.

Alongside the research I captain a **SeaPerch** underwater robotics team, organize
hackathons as a **Hack Club × NASA** Stardance ambassador, and led a youth grant program
that directed $10,000 to local nonprofits.

I am a senior at Millis High School, class of 2027, and I am open to research
collaborations — [get in touch](/contact/).
