    About

    Radar sensing, machine learning systems, and neural interfaces.

    Incoming @ MIT BWSI · SWE @ Synthica · AI research @ Mantis AI (MIT CSAIL) · Rev A @ Solderable · Hack Club × NASA ambassador

    I am a researcher and engineer in Boston working on sensing and machine learning systems. At MIT Lincoln Laboratory's Beaver Works Summer Institute I study synthetic-aperture radar carried on unmanned aircraft; at Mantis AI (MIT CSAIL) I build training-data pipelines for AI systems engineering; at Synthica I maintain research-publication infrastructure; and at Solderable I develop a wireless brain–computer interface platform under a Rev A hardware grant.

    Alongside the research I captain a SeaPerch underwater robotics team, organize hackathons as a Hack Club × NASA Stardance ambassador, and led a youth grant program that directed $10k to local nonprofits.
